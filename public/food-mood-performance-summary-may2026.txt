WHAT IT IS (30 SECONDS)
────────────────────────
Food·Mood is a Spanish-language wellness app for women 45+ that maps emotional
states to functional food recommendations using gut-brain science. Users take an
8-step mood quiz, receive a dominant mood profile (one of 6 states: Activación,
Calma, Focus, Social, Reset, Confort), and get personalised recipe and habit
recommendations — powered by a clinical-grade AI advisor built on top of a
proprietary scientific knowledge base.

The product sits at the intersection of three proven categories:
  · Personalised nutrition (Noom, Lifesum — proved demand, wrong audience)
  · Women's health 45+ (Flo, Gennev — hormones, not gut-brain)
  · AI health coaching (unrealised potential, no Spanish-language leader)

No competitor in Spanish operates at the junction of gut-brain science,
women 45+, and emotionally intelligent AI coaching.

WHY OUR AI IS NOT CHATGPT: THE MOAT EXPLAINED
───────────────────────────────────────────────
Investors often ask: "Can't anyone do this with ChatGPT?" The answer is no —
and understanding why is the key to understanding the defensibility of Food·Mood.

  THE GENERIC AI AGENT (ChatGPT / Claude used raw):
    Trained on all of the internet. Knows a little about everything, a lot
    about nothing specific. Ask it "what should I eat if I slept badly?" and
    you get: "sleep more, eat vegetables, avoid sugar." Correct. Useless.

    It does not know who you are. It does not know you have been in Reset mode
    for 3 days. It does not know Satchin Panda's protocol on chrono-diet and
    short sleep. It does not know your user is 51, perimenopausal, with IBS.
    It has no clinical safety layer. And most critically for an investor:
    any competitor can do exactly the same thing tomorrow, for free.

  THE FOOD·MOOD ASESOR PERSONAL:
    Uses the same base model (Claude Haiku), but before answering it:

      1. Understands who you are (profile, emotional history, biomarkers)
      2. Searches 172 curated scientific fragments for the most relevant
         content for your exact question — using semantic vector similarity
      3. Builds the response combining:
           — what the science says (Cryan, Walker, Panda, Bandura...)
           — what the user knows about herself (today's emotional state)
           — Food·Mood's methodology and brand voice
      4. Passes it through a clinical safety pipeline before delivery

    The same "I slept badly" question becomes:

      "With 4.5h of sleep, your ghrelin is elevated right now — that means
       cravings for calorie-dense food, not real hunger. I suggest a Calma
       day: breakfast with tryptophan (egg + banana), no caffeine after 2pm,
       and if possible, 20 minutes of morning sunlight before 10am to reset
       your cortisol curve."

    That is not ChatGPT. That is a specialist psycho-nutritionist trained on
    your profile, available 24/7 at under €0.003 per consultation.

  WHY THIS MATTERS FOR INVESTORS:

  1. REAL DEFENSIBILITY
     The language model (Claude) is available to anyone. The curated knowledge —
     which books to select, which fragments to index, how to map science to
     emotional states — represents years of the founder's clinical expertise.
     It cannot be replicated in a week or a month.

  2. QUALITY THAT SCALES WITHOUT MARGINAL COST
     Each new book indexed improves every response for every user, permanently,
     with zero additional engineering cost. The knowledge asset appreciates
     over time — like a dataset flywheel.

  3. THE MODEL IS A COMMODITY. THE DATASET IS NOT.
     In applied AI, the model is infrastructure — like electricity. What
     differentiates winners is what you connect it to. The Food·Mood KB
     is the differentiating asset, not Claude.

  4. OPERATING COST 40× LOWER THAN A HUMAN
     A nutritional coach: €60–120/hour.
     The Asesor Personal: €0.003/consultation.
     At a €150–200 LTV per user, the lifetime AI cost is < €0.30.
     The AI line gross margin is > 99%.

  5. A CLINICAL BARRIER GENERIC COMPETITORS CANNOT CROSS
     The safety pipeline (crisis detection, eating disorder signals, allergen
     blocking, drug-food interaction routing) was designed and reviewed by the
     founder as a licensed psychologist. A competitor using a raw API without
     this layer carries clinical and regulatory risk that Food·Mood does not.


INTELLIGENT NOTIFICATION ENGINE
──────────────────────────────────
A rules-based notification system that personalises outreach based on real user
biomarkers — not generic marketing blasts.

  HOW IT WORKS:
    · 3 daily evaluation windows (07:00 / 11:00 / 18:00 UTC)
    · Each user has a notification preference profile:
      — timezone (default: Europe/Madrid)
      — quiet hours (default: 22:00–08:00)
      — per-rule mute controls
    · Rule evaluator checks: quiet hours → already sent today → rule logic
    · Dispatches logged to DB (user_id, rule, channel, timestamp)

  LIVE RULES:
    polyphenol_window   — Fires 15 min before fasting window closes: "break your
                          fast with polyphenols for maximum benefit"
    sleep_short_calm    — If last night's sleep < 6h (from wearable), fires 8-10am:
                          "Calma mode today — no caffeine after 2pm"
    fermented_reminder  — If no fermented food logged in 24h, fires at 13:00:
                          "Your microbiota reminder"
    mood_checkin_nudge  — If no mood check-in today, fires at 20:00:
                          "How did your day end?" (opt-in — off by default)

  CHANNELS: Web Push (VAPID) · Email (Resend) — architecture decoupled,
            SMS/WhatsApp can be added without touching rule logic.
